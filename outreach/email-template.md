# Cold outreach email (lesson 6), HVAC version

Brock's three fixes from the lesson:
1. The subject line is about the **prospect**: their company name and city.
2. Show **proof**: one link to the portfolio example that matches the gap you point out.
3. Make the ask a **low-friction reply**: "reply interested" for one free sample.

That reply is also your filter. Anyone who sends real business details to get the sample is a real lead.

## Template

**Subject:** [Company Name] — [City] heating ads

Hi [First name, if verified; otherwise "Hi there"],

I came across [Company Name]'s [specific current asset: e.g. "Facebook ad for furnace tune-ups that started running in September"] and noticed [one truthful, visible observation: e.g. "it's a single still photo with the phone number in small text"].

I make short video ads for HVAC companies around [region]. Here's one built for a similar problem: [ONE portfolio link that matches the gap].

If it's useful, I'll make **one free sample ad for [Company Name]**, no strings attached. Just **reply "interested"**.

[Your name]
Creative Element · creative-element.net
[Postal address — required]

*This is a commercial message from Creative Element. Reply "stop" and I won't contact you again.*

## Filling rules (from the lesson 6 prompt)
- Leave any field you haven't verified **blank** for the human to fill in. Never guess a first name.
- The observation must be **true, current and verifiable**. Record its URL and the date you saw it in the launch log.
- Include **exactly one** portfolio link. Open it yourself before approving the email.
- Don't invent familiarity ("loved your work!"), results, urgency, consent or company facts.
- Don't comment on how their ads perform. You can only see what the ad looks like, not how it does.

## Good vs weak opening lines

| Weak (mail merge) | Strong (evidence-specific) |
|---|---|
| "I help HVAC companies get more leads with video." | "Your heat-pump ad on Facebook (running since Sept 3) is a stock photo of a unit with no people in it." |
| "Saw your website, great stuff!" | "Your Google Business Profile has 40+ job photos but no video. Those photos could become a 15-second before-and-after." |
| "Your ads aren't performing." *(you can't know that)* | "Your only active Meta ad is a text graphic that's been running since March." |
