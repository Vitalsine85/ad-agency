# Follow-up cadence

Keep it short, honest and limited.

| When | Message |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | Initial email ([email-template.md](email-template.md)) |
| Day 4 | One follow-up (template below) |
| Day 10 | Final note. After that, stop. |

Stop immediately if they say "stop", "not interested" or "unsubscribe", and add them to the suppression list.

**Day 4 follow-up**

> **Subject:** Re: [Company Name] — [City] heating ads
>
> Hi [name / there]. Quick nudge in case this got buried. The free sample ad for [Company Name] is still on offer. Just reply "interested" and I'll send five quick questions.
>
> [signature, postal address, opt-out line]

**Day 10 final note**

> **Subject:** Re: [Company Name] — [City] heating ads
>
> Last note from me, I promise. If video ads ever make sense for [Company Name], reply "interested" anytime. Thanks, and good luck with the [season] rush.
>
> [signature, postal address, opt-out line]

## Handling replies

| Reply | Action |
|---|---|
| "interested" (or clearly means yes) | The reply rule drafts the intake email ([ops/reply-trigger.md](../ops/reply-trigger.md)). A human approves and sends it. |
| A question ("how much?", "who are you?") | Answer it honestly, then give the free-sample next step. The pricing hypothesis is in [business/offer.md](../business/offer.md). Don't quote a price before you've confirmed it. |
| "Not now" | Thank them, and ask if it's OK to check back next season. Only follow up if they say yes. |
| "Stop" / "unsubscribe" / "not interested" | Add them to the suppression list the same day. Don't reply with a sales pitch. |
