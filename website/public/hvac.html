Questions

The things HVAC owners ask first.

Is the sample really free?

Yes. One sample ad, no contract, no card. We need a few real details from you to make it, which is also how we know you're seriously considering it.

Will it look fake or "AI"?

We review every clip frame by frame and throw out anything with obvious AI glitches. Your logo, phone number and offer are added by us in editing, not generated, so they're always exact.

Do I have to be on camera?

No. If you'd like your team or trucks in the ads, send photos or clips and we'll work them in. Otherwise we create the scenes.

Will you make claims about my business I haven't approved?

No. Prices, rebates, financing, warranties, response times, licenses, years in business: none of it goes in an ad unless you've given it to us and approved the wording. People shown are actors or AI, never presented as your real customers.

Do you run the ads for me?

We make the ads and deliver them in every format you need. You or your ad manager run them in your Meta and Google accounts. Ad spend is separate.

What happens after the sample?

If you like it, we'll offer a small fixed-price pilot batch. After that, you can choose new ad variations every week or every other week. If not, you keep the sample and that's it.

Who owns the ads?

Once paid for, the finished ads are yours to use in your marketing. The free sample is yours to keep either way.
