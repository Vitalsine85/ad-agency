# Intake fact rules: what is claim-critical and what to do when it's missing

From [course lesson 5](../course/lessons/05-design-the-client-intake.md), adapted for HVAC.

Build the intake backwards from the finished ad: **collect a fact only if it could change the copy, the visuals, the offer, or where the ad is delivered.**

## Two questions for every fact
1. **Where did it come from?** One of:
   - client-supplied
   - a public URL plus the date we looked at it
   - an inference, with its stated basis
   - missing
2. **Who approved its use?** One of:
   - unconfirmed
   - client-confirmed
   - approved by the named approver
   - a flagged assumption

Seeing a fact on a public website confirms it exists. **It never gives us permission to publish it in an ad.**

## Fact table (HVAC)

| Fact | Acceptable source | If missing | Claim-critical |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business name and city | Client, or a dated public URL | Keep provisional until the client confirms | Yes |
| Service area (towns) | Client | Stop and ask | Yes |
| Services offered and services to feature | Client | Stop and ask | Yes |
| Phone, email, booking link | Client | **Stop and ask** | Yes |
| Offer, promotion, deadline | Client | **Stop and ask** (or run with no offer) | Yes |
| Price, financing terms | Client | **Stop and ask**. Never invent | Yes |
| Rebates and incentives (Mass Save, NHSaves, Efficiency Vermont, federal) | Client **plus** a dated official program URL | **Stop and ask**. Never estimate amounts | Yes |
| License and registration numbers (MA gas fitter, sheet metal, HIC) | Client | Leave out of the ad unless the client supplies them | Yes |
| Warranties, guarantees, "same-day", "24/7", years in business | Client | **Stop and ask**, or leave the claim out | Yes |
| Required disclaimers | Client, or an approved legal source | **Stop and ask** | Yes |
| Logo and brand assets | Client, as files | **Stop and ask**. Never recreate a logo from a screenshot | Yes |
| Brand colors and fonts | Client, or inference from their site | Mark as an assumption | No |
| Real photos or footage (trucks, techs, jobs) | Client | Use generic AI scenes with no client branding on people | No |
| Target audience and creative angle | Client, or a stated inference | Mark as an assumption | No |
| Things to avoid | Client | Ask; the default is none known | No |
| Delivery destination (email, Drive folder, ad account) | Client | **Stop and ask** | Yes |

## When generation may start
- **Free sample:** every claim-critical fact that will *appear in that sample* is client-confirmed. Any creative assumptions are clearly marked.
- **Paid batch:** the whole profile has been reviewed with the client, and the named approver has approved it.
