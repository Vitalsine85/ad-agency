# Free-sample intake: "five quick questions" (sent after a prospect replies "interested")

This is the email the reply rule drafts ([ops/reply-trigger.md](../ops/reply-trigger.md)). **A human approves it before it's sent.**
The five questions cover everything a single free sample needs. The full onboarding questions further down are only for paying clients.

---

**Subject:** Your free sample ad — 5 quick questions

Hi [FIRST_NAME or "there"],

Thanks for replying. To make your free sample ad, I just need five things. Short answers are fine:

1. **What should the ad promote?** For example: heating tune-ups, heat-pump installs, AC replacement, a maintenance plan.
2. **What phone number or booking link should the ad show?**
3. **Is there an offer, price or deadline you want in it?** If you'd rather not include one, say "no offer".
4. **Which towns do you serve?**
5. **Can you attach your logo** (PNG or SVG)? A couple of real photos of your trucks or team are a bonus.

I'll only use what you send me. I won't add prices, rebates, guarantees or claims you haven't approved. You'll see the ad before anything else happens. It's yours to keep either way.

[YOUR_NAME]
Creative Element · creative-element.net
[POSTAL_ADDRESS]
Don't want to hear from me again? Reply "stop" and I won't email you again.

## Full onboarding (paying clients only)

Collect these in addition to the five questions. Record each one in the profile with its source and approval status ([client-profile.template.md](client-profile.template.md)).

**Business identity**
- Legal and trading name
- Website
- Google Business Profile URL
- Years in business (only if they want it shown)

**Licensing**
- Licenses and registrations they want displayed: MA gas fitter, sheet metal, HIC; NH/VT equivalents

**Services and hours**
- Full service list
- Brands they install
- Emergency or after-hours service: yes or no, and the exact wording they want

**Offers and terms**
- Financing (lender name and approved wording)
- Warranties
- Guarantees
- Rebate participation, with a link to the official program page

**Brand**
- Logo files
- Colors
- Fonts
- Tone
- Words or claims to avoid
- Competitors they don't want to resemble

**Assets**
- Real photos and video, with permission to use them in ads
- Whether staff appear in that footage, and whether they've consented

**Ads and reporting**
- Ad platforms and ad account access (only if we're delivering into the account)
- Who approves ads
- Where to deliver files
- What performance data they'll share each week (CTR, CPL, calls)
