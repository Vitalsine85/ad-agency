# Role: Cinematic before/after

**Job:** show a visible transformation. **Route:** generate an image for the "before" frame and another for the "after" frame (same angle), then use `generate_video` image-to-video with the before frame as the start and the after frame as the end. Add a voiceover or text overlay in post.

**Prompt (video):**
> 15-second vertical 9:16 ad for a residential HVAC company in rural New England. 0–3s HOOK: tight shot of a rusted, frost-covered old outdoor AC/heat unit beside a white clapboard farmhouse in late-autumn light, breath visible in the cold air — on-screen text: "Still running this?" 3–10s: smooth time-lapse transition, same camera angle, to a clean, modern, compact heat-pump unit on a fresh pad, same house now warmly lit inside at dusk. 10–15s: interior, family relaxing in a warm living room, a ductless head unit on the wall. Cinematic, natural color, shallow depth of field, no visible brand logos, no text other than the hook. Leave the final 3 seconds uncluttered for an end card.

**End card (post):** [Client logo] · "[CTA, e.g. Book a free heat-pump estimate]" · [phone] · [URL] — client-supplied only.
**Never:** imply this is the client's real job unless it is; no rebate or savings numbers.
