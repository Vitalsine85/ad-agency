# Role: Problem-focused

**Job:** a specific cost or inconvenience. **Route:** `ugc-video` product/voiceover format (product = the service) or `generate_video` + voiceover.

**Prompt:**
> 15-second vertical 9:16 ad. 0–3s HOOK: close-up of a thermostat reading 58°F with a hand tapping it, text: "Heat's on. House is still cold?" 3–10s: quick, honest shots — a loud rattling old blower, a space heater in a kid's room, a frosty window. Voiceover (calm, local, adult): "Uneven heat, strange noises, a system that runs nonstop — those are signs it needs attention before the next cold snap." 10–15s: a technician (generic uniform, no logos) checking a unit in a clean basement. Leave last 3s for an end card.

**End card (post):** [logo] · "[CTA, e.g. Schedule a heating check]" · [phone] · [service area towns].
**Never:** promise energy savings %, response times or prices unless client-confirmed.
