# Portfolio batch prompt (lesson 4)

## Course prompt, verbatim, with the niche filled in

> Make five video ads for **residential HVAC contractors in Western Massachusetts and southern New England**, each in a completely different style: cinematic before-and-after, funny, a clearly labeled fictional customer scenario, problem-focused, and premium brand. Use the audience and offer already defined. Give every ad a strong hook in the first three seconds and a clear call to action at the end. Do not fabricate a testimonial or imply that an actor or AI avatar is a real customer.

**Context to give Claude alongside the prompt.** Attach or paste:
- [`business/offer.md`](../../business/offer.md)
- the fictional demo client below
- the role prompts in this folder

## Selecting the final five
- Generate a couple of options per role if the budget allows.
- Keep the clearest one for each role.
- If two finalists cover the same role, drop one and regenerate the missing role.

The portfolio is ready when each clip gives a prospect a *different* reason to believe Creative Element can handle their brief.

| Role | What the chosen ad must show |
|---|---|
| Before/after | A visible transformation (an old rusted condenser becomes a clean, quiet heat pump) |
| Problem-led | A specific cost or inconvenience (no heat on the coldest night, a loud old unit) |
| Fictional scenario | A relatable situation, clearly labeled as a dramatization |
| Funny | A memorable hook that still fits a trustworthy trade brand |
| Premium | Visual and verbal choices that support a higher-end position |
