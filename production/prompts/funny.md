# Role: Funny

**Job:** a memorable hook that still fits a trustworthy trade brand. **Route:** `generate_video` (Seedance/Kling class) or `ugc-video` productless story.

**Prompt:**
> 15-second vertical 9:16 comedic ad. 0–3s HOOK: a man in a New England kitchen wearing a winter parka, hat and mittens, solemnly eating cereal while his breath fogs — text: "Day 3 of 'the furnace is fine.'" 3–9s: quick cuts — the dog in a sweater giving him a look; his partner handing him a phone with a dial screen already open. 9–15s: same kitchen, warm and bright, him in a t-shirt, relieved, dog asleep by a floor vent. Light, warm comedic tone, natural acting, no dialogue except optional one line: "Okay. I'm calling." No logos, no on-screen phone numbers. Leave the last 3s for an end card.

**End card (post):** [logo] · "Don't wait for day 3. [CTA]" · [phone].
**Keep it kind:** joke about the situation, never mock the homeowner, competitors or a demographic.
