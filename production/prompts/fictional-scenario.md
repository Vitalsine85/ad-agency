# Role: Fictional customer scenario (clearly labeled)

**Job:** a relatable situation — NOT a testimonial. **Route:** `ugc-video` (review format / productless creator story). The ugc-video workflow forbids testimonials and impersonation; keep it that way.

**On-screen label (required, whole duration, legible):** "Dramatization — fictional scenario"

**Script prompt:**
> 20-second vertical 9:16 creator-style video, adult homeowner in their 40s talking to camera in a mudroom, natural daylight, handheld feel. Native audio. Script (spoken): "So last January our old boiler quit at 2 a.m. It was nine degrees out. Here's what I'd tell anyone in that spot: don't wait for the coldest night of the year to find out. Get it looked at in the fall." Final 3 seconds: they glance at a phone, nod. No product claims, no prices, no company names spoken, no dollar figures.

**Rules (course lesson 4 + FTC):** no specific losses or results presented as real; no "I used [Client] and…"; the person is an actor/AI and must not be presented as a real customer. If the client later has a genuine, consenting customer, use their real, approved account instead.
