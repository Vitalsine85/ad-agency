# Creative Element — HVAC offer (Lesson 2)

> First service line of the Creative Element studio (see [README](README.md)). The studio will grow into broader creative services for local brands; this offer is deliberately narrow so it can be tested and measured.

**Agency:** Creative Element · creative-element.net · based in Orange, MA
**Niche:** residential HVAC contractors (heating, cooling, heat pumps)
**Service area:** North Quabbin, Franklin County, north Worcester County, Pioneer Valley, Keene NH (Monadnock), Brattleboro VT

Method: [course lesson 2](../course/lessons/02-shape-the-offer.md) — five decisions, measurable promises, no invented proof, guarantees, scarcity, or results.

## Thesis (from Lesson 1)
We will test HVAC because ~$128 search cost-per-lead and $1,500–1,800/mo median ad spend show real budgets, Google's move of Local Services Ads into Performance Max (which auto-generates video when none is uploaded) leaves a specific creative gap, and New England heating breakdowns, heat-pump conversions and summer AC replacements (~$7,500 avg) are visible, urgent buyer outcomes. — sources in [01-niche-research.md](01-niche-research.md)

## Deliverable vs outcome

| Deliverable (what we make) | Outcome (what the owner wants) |
|---|---|
| Short video and static ads for Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and Google Performance Max | A steady flow of service calls and replacement estimates — especially to keep techs and install crews booked in the shoulder seasons |

"Designed to bring in estimate requests" — never "will bring". We don't control their ad budget, targeting, offer or phone answering.

## Pricing — assumptions, not facts
The course's $500 test / $1,000-per-video / $2,000-per-month figures are **presenter examples, not market rates** (lessons 2, 8, 12). The numbers below are **starting hypotheses** to be validated in [`finance/economics-model.xlsx`](../finance/economics-model.xlsx) before quoting anyone:

| Item | Hypothesis | Validate by |
|---|---|---|
| Free sample | 1 static or short video ad; direct cost ≈ a few credits + ~20 min review | Log actual credits + minutes per sample |
| Pilot | 5 ads, fixed price `$[PILOT_PRICE]` (test band $400–$750) | Paid conversion from sample-ready profiles |
| Subscription | see [subscription.md](subscription.md) | Renewal after first month |

Set the price only after one full sample + pilot cycle has been costed (credits, review time, revisions, payment fees).

## Hard "never" list for HVAC ads
- No invented rebate amounts, tax credits, financing terms, or "Mass Save approved" claims — **only** what the client supplies with a dated official source.
- No license/registration numbers, warranties, guarantees ("100% satisfaction"), response times ("same-day", "24/7") or years in business unless client-confirmed.
- No synthetic "customer" presented as real. Fictional scenarios carry an on-screen "Dramatization" label.
- Financing ads may fall under Meta's Financial Products special ad category — flag to the client.
