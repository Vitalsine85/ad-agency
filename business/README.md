## Why start with one narrow offer
The course's method is: pick one evidence-backed niche, test it with 10–15 qualified prospects, then go, revise or stop based on observed numbers. A broad "we do everything" pitch is hard to measure and easy to ignore. The HVAC line proves out the shared machinery that every later service line reuses:
- intake with provenance tracking
- the QC gate
- human approval
- the subscription operations
- the economics model

## Brand rules that apply to every service line
The [course synthesis](../course/SYNTHESIS.md) lists 10 non-negotiable operating rules. Every Creative Element service follows them:
- no fabricated proof
- client-approved facts only
- named human approval before anything ships
- an honest pricing rationale
