# Niche research (Lesson 1) — local & regional US

Research date: 2026-10-15. Method: [course/lessons/01](../course/lessons/01-find-an-underserved-niche.md) — budget already exists + visible creative gap + urgent buyer outcome, every number cited.

**How to read the scores (1–5 each, /25 total)**
- (a) Ad spend — LocalIQ benchmarks, Google Local Services Ads (LSA) eligibility.
- (b) Ability to pay — job size, revenue per firm (IBISWorld market size ÷ firm count).
- (c) Urgency — analyst judgment (emergency / storm / seasonal), not a sourced statistic.
- (d) Creative gap — **provisional everywhere**: no published study measures local ad creative quality. Must be confirmed with the Meta Ad Library audit below before launch.
- (e) Growth — IBISWorld US revenue CAGR 2021–26.

- **[LHS25]** LocalIQ, *Home Services Search Advertising Benchmarks* (3,211 campaigns, Apr 2024–Mar 2025, medians) — https://localiq.com/blog/home-services-search-advertising-benchmarks/
- **[LHS21]** LocalIQ, *Home Services Advertising Benchmarks* (median monthly spend; 2020–21, dated) — https://localiq.com/blog/home-services-advertising-benchmarks/
- **[L26]** LocalIQ/WordStream, *Search Advertising Benchmarks 2026* — https://localiq.com/blog/search-advertising-benchmarks/

- **[IBIS]** IBISWorld US industry pages — `https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/market-research-reports/<industry>-industry/`
- **[PMAX]** Google Ads Help: Performance Max auto-generates a video if the advertiser uploads none — https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14528532
- **[MIG]** Search Engine Journal (2026): LSA moving into Google Ads as Performance Max pay-per-lead (Search & Maps only) from Aug 2026 — https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-is-bringing-local-services-ads-into-google-ads/582816/

**Why there's a plausible creative gap in the trades:** LSA ads are text-only; video for these businesses has to run on Meta, YouTube, Performance Max and CTV; and Performance Max fills in auto-generated video when none is uploaded [PMAX]. That's a structural reason to expect weak video — **not proof**. Verify per metro.

## Recommended top 3 (solo operator, local/regional)

PI law was excluded despite scoring 20: smallest provable gap, entrenched TV agencies, bar-advertising rules that are especially risky for AI-generated people.

1. **Roofing (24)** — "We will test roofing because the highest home-services search CPL ($228) and LSA eligibility show spend, text-only LSA ads plus Google auto-generating video for roofers who upload none show a specific creative gap, and storm/hail replacement demand on ~$10k jobs is a visible urgent buyer outcome."
2. **HVAC (22)** — "We will test HVAC because ~$128 CPLs and $1,500–1,800/mo median spend show spend, LSA's migration into Performance Max (auto-video if none uploaded) leaves a creative gap, and seasonal breakdowns and ~$7,500 replacements are a visible urgent buyer outcome."
3. **Plumbing (22) — or HVAC+plumbing combo shops** — "We will test plumbing because a $129 CPL and the highest median spend ($2,500/mo) show spend, mostly-text LSA/search with auto-generated video shows a creative gap, and emergency calls are a visible urgent outcome." Caveat: emergency buyers search rather than scroll; sell video for water-heater/repipe upsells, memberships and recruiting. **Alternate:** garage door (19) — very visual before/after, $4,672 replacement.

## Compliance flags by niche
- **Roofing:** Colorado ([C.R.S. 6-22-105](https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_6-22-105)) and Texas (HB 2102) prohibit advertising deductible waivers/payments. Financing ads may require Meta's Financial Products special ad category. FTC fake reviews & testimonials rule (2024) bans fake/AI customer testimonials ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/ftc-fake-reviews-testimonials-445209)).
- **Med spa / dental:** never generate before/after "results"; substantiation and FDA/FTC risk.
- **PI law:** dramatization/actor disclaimers (state bars); AI "lawyer" likely misleading.
- **Real estate / financing:** Meta special ad categories restrict targeting.

## Choosing a launch metro
Criteria: ≥150 target businesses (Census County Business Patterns — NAICS 238160 roofing, 238220 plumbing/HVAC); active advertisers in the Meta Ad Library; a demand trigger (hail/storm belt, extreme heat); job size vs cost of living ([BEA RPP](https://www.bea.gov/data/prices-inflation/regional-price-parities-state-and-metro-area)); moderate agency saturation (unverified — check who runs the ads).

## Meta Ad Library audit (do this before committing)
facebook.com/ads/library → United States, All ads → search "roofing", "roof replacement", "AC repair" → Active, Media type = Videos then Images. For ~30 advertisers in the metro record: video share, 3-second hook (y/n), captions, 9:16, specific offer, stock vs real footage, run length, number of live variations. Repeat for YouTube at adstransparency.google.com. Log results in `launch/launch-log.csv`.

## Claims not verified (excluded from scoring)
- **"Roofing grows 6%/yr" — refuted for US revenue.** IBISWorld: 4.9%/yr 2021–26, −1.3% in 2026. The 6% likely comes from BLS roofer *employment* growth over 10 years (2024–34) ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/roofers.htm)).
- The creative gap itself (no study found).
- Per-trade LSA cost-per-lead (agency datasets only); first-wave LSA→PMax categories (one secondary source).
- Home services marketing as 8–12% of revenue (vendor claims only).
- Med spa per-location revenue; dental implant prices; PI case values.
- Florida lawyer dramatization wording in force; any FTC/state rule specific to AI-generated people.
- Metro cooling demand, agency saturation, a current metro hail ranking (NICB data is 2017–19).
- LocalIQ monthly-spend medians are from 2020–21.
