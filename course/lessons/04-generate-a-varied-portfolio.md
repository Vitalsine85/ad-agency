## Lesson review

A portfolio should show range, not five variations of the same idea. Decide which creative roles you need before generating the batch. In the video, those roles are a before-and-after, a funny concept, a testimonial-style story, a problem-led ad, and a premium brand treatment.


The portfolio request being written in Claude.

Because there is no real client or customer in this exercise, replace the testimonial with a clearly labeled fictional scenario:

**Prompt (copy this asset):**

Make five video ads for [niche], each in a completely different style: cinematic before-and-after, funny, a clearly labeled fictional customer scenario, problem-focused, and premium brand. Use the audience and offer already defined. Give every ad a strong hook in the first three seconds and a clear call to action at the end. Do not fabricate a testimonial or imply that an actor or AI avatar is a real customer.

## Review each ad for its intended job

The second clip is a good example of why generation still needs editorial review. A synthetic speaker describes a specific $12,000 loss, but the source provides no real customer or substantiation for that story.

Label the scenario as fiction and remove unsupported specifics, or replace it with a genuine, approved customer account. The FTC’s [testimonial guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers) makes clear that a dramatization can still mislead when viewers are likely to read it as a real experience.

## Select for coverage

Creative role| What the selected ad should demonstrate  
---|---  
Before-and-after| A visible transformation  
Problem-led| A specific cost or inconvenience  
Fictional scenario| A relatable situation, clearly labeled  
Funny| A memorable hook that still fits the brand  
Premium| Visual and verbal choices that support a higher-end position  
  

The presenter comparing the generated options before choosing a final five.

If two finalists serve the same role, keep the clearer one and regenerate the missing role. The portfolio is ready when each clip gives a prospect a different reason to believe you can handle their brief.
